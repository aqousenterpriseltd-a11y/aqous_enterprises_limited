Services

01

Architectural plans

Drawings taken from concept through to approval-ready plans, shaped around how you'll actually use the space.

02

Structural drawings

Engineered structural designs and detailing for foundations, framing and roofing, sized correctly for the site.

03

Geotechnical services

Soil investigation and site assessment before design begins, so foundations are specified for the ground you're building on.

Process

Five phases, tracked from start to handover

Every build is broken into the same five phases so you always know what's finished, what's underway, and what it's costing you at each stage.

1

Foundation

Site clearance, setting out and substructure works.

2

Structure

Walling, columns, beams and the roof structure.

3

Envelope

Roofing, windows, doors and external rendering.

4

Finishes

Plumbing, wiring, tiling, painting and fittings.

5

Handover

Snagging, inspection sign-off and keys handed over.
